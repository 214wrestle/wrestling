## Michael Moreno — Iowa State, collegiate 2013–2015

Primary photo source: https://cyclones.com/sports/wrestling/roster/michael-moreno/1333

Visually inspected the official NCAA 2014–15 hand-raise photograph and portrait on that page. The action image shows a lean muscular frame, short dark hair, cardinal singlet with gold edging and white headgear. The portrait supports the tapered lower face, dark eyebrows and short hair. The archived roster page mixes collegiate photos with a later graduate-assistant biography; do not treat its suit portrait as match-era gear.

Implemented a photo-guided procedural approximation, including frame, face, hair and uniform colours. Height (1.77 m), geometry ratios, skin/eye colours are art estimates, not verified measurements. The model remains stylized and does not yet achieve a convincing realistic likeness. Motion uses the default profile; match footage must be reviewed before assigning signature technique tendencies.

Collegiate record cross-reference: https://nwhof.org/national-wrestling-hall-of-fame/champions-database?tab=ncaa&wrestler=16086

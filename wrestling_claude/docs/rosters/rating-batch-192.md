# Ben Lewis — career 192

Era-normalized three varsity opportunities, 1970–1972. The 1972 NCAA guide describes him as a junior in 1971; official MSU letterwinner table includes 1970 and 1972. Greater Flint Area Sports Hall of Fame confirms high school football through 1967 and MSU collegiate wrestling/judo through 1972. His Iowa State football commitment was withdrawn before attending, not a transfer college wrestling season.

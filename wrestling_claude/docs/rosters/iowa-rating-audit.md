Reconciliations: Jim Zalesky's guide has inconsistent 132–7–1 and 131-win fields. Use NWHOF's internally consistent season table and career 131–8–1, also matching Iowa's career-record list. Ed Banach's 1983 guide row repeats a tie that the career total excludes; NWHOF identifies 34–3–0 and career 141–9–1. Barry Davis guide text lists two ties but Navy's official biography and Iowa career list give 162–9–1; use the latter. Jeff McGinness's retrospective biography reverses the 1996/1997 redshirt chronology; NWHOF season table and Iowa letterwinner list agree on competing 1996, redshirting 1997. The [1996 bracket](https://nwhof.org/brackets/66#page=6) confirms McGinness competed and lost to Guerrero in the round of 12; this DNW earns the qualifier point. Mark Perry's 2006 Gorriaran award is not NCAA Outstanding Wrestler and earns no bonus. Mark Ironside's Hodge award earns no NCAA OW bonus.

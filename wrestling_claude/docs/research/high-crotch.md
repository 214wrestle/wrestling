# High-crotch prototype

The game now distinguishes the high crotch from the low single and double: an inside penetration step, higher near-thigh grip, outside head position, trail-leg recovery and a rising corner finish. Each has paired authored poses and grip contacts; success still requires winning the contested-leg battle. Athlete shot weights now affect the choice, with angles favoring near-leg attacks. Unprofiled athletes retain the previous double/single selection.

Technique reference: [USA Wrestling Seven Basic Skills guide](https://cdn2.sportngin.com/attachments/document/0119/7259/7-Basic-Skills-Guide_for_Wrestlers.pdf#page=5), which identifies the high-crotch drill at 28:23–28:50 and describes driving up and through with the head up, back straight and trail leg brought forward. The written curriculum informed this first animation; it is not motion capture or a completed film-analysis reconstruction.

# NCAA bracket evidence

Use the [user-supplied NWCA Division I bracket archive](https://nwcaonline.com/sports/2023/11/9/ncaa-division-i-men-nationals-bracket-archive) as a primary source for tournament participants, final placements and individual bout outcomes. `nwca-bracket-index.json` records the 94 years with bracket links observed on October 5, 2026, from 1928 through 2025. An archive index is not a parsed bout dataset and does not imply that every year or result is available.

Match roster athletes by full name, school, year and actual tournament weight. Save the PDF page with each qualifier/placement result. Reconcile abbreviated names against school biographies before merging identities; retain historical weights as evidence while preserving the owner's game roster assignments. Do not infer a nonqualifier solely from a failed text search of a scanned bracket.

For later head-to-head analysis retain both athletes, date/year, round, weight, score and finish type, plus PDF page. Falls/defaults without final numerical scores do not enter scoring averages. Distinguish tournament Outstanding Wrestler from other awards. Account for changed scoring, consolation format and eligibility rules across eras before comparing rates.

Head-to-head evidence may help validate era peers and move tendencies, but it does not yet change gameplay ratings. Tournament opponents are a selected sample, and NCAA brackets omit dual meets and other collegiate matches. Avoid double-counting the same win through placement points, career win percentage and a head-to-head bonus.
